Privacy

Updated 17 September 2026

Research Trail is operated by Manan Parihar. This notice explains the current service. For questions or a data-deletion request, email mananparihar03@gmail.com.

Your account

Google sign-in shares basic identity information with Supabase, our authentication service: your email address, name, profile image, and Google account identifier. We use this to identify your account and keep your learning progress separate from other users. The app displays your name.

Research Trail does not receive your Google password. It requests basic identity, email, and profile access—not access to Gmail or Google Drive.

Learning progress and browser storage

Signed-in concept familiarity answers, prerequisite-resource checkmarks, and the paper reading statuses you choose (Reading or Finished) are stored in Supabase and cached in your browser to support syncing. Your profile summarizes these self-reported choices, not proof of mastery; opening a paper does not record completion or measure understanding.

Guest progress stays in that browser. It remains separate from account progress and is not automatically imported when you sign in. The browser also stores sign-in session information and local preferences.

This site runs under manan03.github.io. Browser storage is shared at that origin, not isolated by the /research-trail-site/ path. Clearing that origin’s site data may affect other sites hosted there.

Paper conversations

The hosted AI tutor is currently unavailable while its model connection is being configured. Once enabled, asking a question will send your question, bounded conversation history, and the paper’s curator-written guide to Groq for a model response. Google profile details and learning-progress records are not intentionally included in that model request. Do not include sensitive information in a question.

The tutor stores account- and paper-linked conversations in Supabase, keeping up to six question-and-answer pairs per conversation. Access expires after 30 minutes of inactivity. Expired records are cleaned up during later requests; expiry is not a guarantee of deletion at that time. Account-linked usage counters help enforce request limits.

Services and external links

GitHub Pages hosts the website. Supabase provides authentication, the progress database, and the tutor backend. These providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, request details, and service logs to operate their services. Their own retention and processing practices also apply.

Paper links, embedded PDFs, videos, repositories, and other learning resources connect to external websites. Those websites receive the requests and apply their own privacy policies.

Provider notices: Google, Supabase, GitHub, and Groq.

Your choices

  • Read as a guest without creating an account.
  • Log out to end this browser’s signed-in session. Logout does not delete your account or saved cloud progress.
  • Clear this origin’s browser site data to remove local guest progress, preferences, caches, and session information. Cloud records remain.
  • Revoke Research Trail’s access in your Google Account’s connected-app settings. Revocation does not itself delete Research Trail data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data at mananparihar03@gmail.com. Account and cloud-progress records are retained until deletion is requested and handled. There is no automatic account-expiry schedule.

We will update this notice when the service’s data handling changes.
